## 💡 Top 5 Practical Recording Tips for Suneet

1. **Pre-record clean screen captures separately:**
   - Don't record your webcam and screen live in one take if you get nervous.
   - Record 45 seconds of your Desktop UI (zooming into the 3D map and Groq chat).
   - Record 30 seconds of your Android phone screen.
   - Then record your face/voiceover in OBS or Premiere/CapCut and sync them.
2. **Show, Don't Just Tell:**
   - When you say *"elevated flyovers"*, let the mouse hover over the green safe path line.
   - When you say *"Groq Copilot"*, show the chat typing out the response.
   - When you mention the APK, show the app opening from the phone home screen.
3. **Keep an Eye on the 3:00 Clock:**
   - Hackathon platforms (Devpost, Unstop, YouTube submissions) automatically disqualify or penalize videos that run even 1 second over 3:00 (e.g. 3:01).  
   - **Aim for 2:50 to 2:55.**
4. **Audio Clarity is 70% of the Video:**
   - Use your best microphone/earphones in a quiet room with minimal echo.
   - Background music should be very subtle (lo-fi ambient tech beat at -25dB).
5. **Add the Live URL on Screen:**
   - In the lower-third banner throughout the video, keep a small, elegant pill:  
     `🌐 Live Deployment: https://d38z2t6y9aj34z.cloudfront.net`
